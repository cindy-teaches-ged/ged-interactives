How to copy your draft into Google Docs — step by step
  1. Click the green Copy to clipboard button below. Your draft will be copied automatically.
  2. Open a new browser tab. In the address bar at the top of your screen, type docs.new and press Enter. This opens a blank Google Doc — a free online word processor that saves automatically to your Google account.
  3. Click anywhere inside the blank white document page.
  4. Hold the Ctrl key and press V at the same time. Your draft will appear.
  5. Your work saves automatically. You can close the tab and find it again anytime by going to drive.google.com.
